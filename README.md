# CITY OF SHADOWS

**A 3D African open-world street-survival and crime-drama game.**

> A city. A thousand secrets. One fight for power.

## Created by Dsquare

**CITY OF SHADOWS is an original game project created by Dsquare.** It is a standalone project, separate from Petros Curious Mind.

## The vision

A fictional modern African city inspired by the energy and variety of Lagos. The player arrives with very little and builds a life through exploration, work, relationships, survival, and dangerous choices.

## Current playable foundation

The browser build uses JavaScript and Three.js and is designed with low-memory Android devices in mind.

- Character creation with male/female selection, age, skin tone, hairstyle and selectable glasses, gold chain and wristwatch accessories.
- Third-person neighbourhood exploration with touch joystick and action buttons, plus PC keyboard controls.
- A low-rise neighbourhood with roads, pavements, buildings, windows, trees and streetlights.
- A 24/7 corner shop and a street market with produce stalls, separate proximity prompts and separate item catalogues.
- Parked low-poly vehicles that can be entered, driven and exited, with simple moving traffic and collision checks.
- Walking pedestrians and a sparring opponent.
- Punch and kick targeting for nearby pedestrians and the sparring opponent.
- A local wallet and inventory, food and water use, and clothing purchases.
- Separate corner-shop and City Market catalogues, with fresh food, first-aid kits and different item effects.
- Saved character accessories appear on the in-game player model; purchased clothing changes the player's outfit.
- A repeatable Market Delivery job that rewards ₦120 when the player reaches the marked market on foot; job earnings are saved locally.
- A physically enterable safehouse with a rest point that restores energy, hunger and hydration.
- A working vehicle horn on the in-car controls, plus pedestrians who return to their routes after a cooldown.
- First-aid kits restore up to 45 health, and defeated sparring opponents return after a short cooldown.
- A survival-needs HUD for hunger, hydration and energy. Hunger and hydration gradually decrease during play; energy drains while running and recovers while walking or standing. Low energy reduces movement speed, and food/water restore needs.
- Wardrobe slots for tops, trousers and shoes. Hoodie, denim jacket, blue jeans and sneakers can be purchased; equipped items change the prototype character's material colours.
- In-city bag/wardrobe access and a pause menu.
- Local browser storage for character, wallet, inventory, equipment and needs.

## Controls

**Android:** virtual movement joystick, punch, kick, run, block and dodge buttons. Enter a parked or moving car to use GO, BACK, HORN and EXIT; use the dedicated steering joystick to turn and the pedals to accelerate or reverse. Drag the 3D view to rotate the camera.

**PC:** WASD or arrow keys to move, Shift or M to run, E to enter/exit a nearby car, W/S to drive, A/D to steer, and mouse drag to look around. Press I for the inventory.

## Development roadmap

The next major systems are a larger connected city, more distinctive buildings and districts, richer character models and clothing meshes, expanded jobs and mission chains, police response, rival groups, ranged combat, properties, businesses, relationships, reputation, a map, day/night, sound, and more complete save/progression systems.

These are planned features, not claims about what is already playable. The current character models and environment are deliberately simple low-poly prototypes.

## Performance and deployment

The game is deployed as a browser project on GitHub Pages. It needs a modern browser, WebGL and an internet connection to load the Three.js module. It is not yet an offline Android app or a finished PC executable.

The project is being expanded in coherent playable milestones, with performance kept in mind for low-memory Android hardware.
